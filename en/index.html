About

Elena Luppol is a Candidate of Economic Sciences and Associate Professor at the Department of Finance and Economic Security of Donetsk National Technical University. She graduated from the same university with honours as an engineer-economist, with a thorough grounding in both engineering and economics.

Her research is the modelling, optimisation and forecasting of financial and economic processes. She develops economic-mathematical methods and models that measure the level of financial and economic security of an industrial enterprise and build scenarios for its further development, and she has worked with the financial and economic data of industrial enterprises for more than five years.

Since 2022 she has been developing software in Java: she completed the Java Developer professional training programme at Skypro, worked on team projects, and continues to grow in the profession.
